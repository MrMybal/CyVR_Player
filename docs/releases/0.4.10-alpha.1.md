# CyVR Player 0.4.10 Alpha 1

Display → Video format now has a persistent Auto-detection On/Off control. Detection is off by default, including on upgrades. Opening another video keeps manual format selections when disabled. Detect once applies the current file's hints without enabling the preference. Manual format controls remain available in both modes.

VR180 now supports 0.25×–4× zoom through the buttons, slider, right stick and grip + trigger gesture. Zooming out reveals the environment around the captured picture instead of stretching its edges, including the native equirectangular compositor layer. Native, CAS, FSR and GSR retain separate eye sampling. Fit image restores 1×; cinema/360° retain their previous zoom limits.

The setting and VR180 zoom survive host replacement. Synthetic Vulkan checks cover small zoom, horizontal positioning, mono/SBS/TB, both eyes and the rear hemisphere. Physical controller comfort and the preferred zoom level still need worn-headset validation.
